Repeatable Schedules

Build the blueprint once.
Stamp it onto any day.

Instead of typing out every recurring task across 30 days, save routines as master blueprints. In Remind Me, applying a whole day takes a single click.

  • Reusable Template Library: Save routines like "Break, Coding, and Fitness" with pre-set intervals and time limits.
  • One-Click Apply: Select a template from the mint sidebar and click any calendar day to instantly populate it.
  • Non-Destructive Tweaks: Modify individual days without breaking your underlying master template.

Word Completer

1000+ Word Dictionary.
Less clicking, zero typing drag.

As you type in a block name, Remind Me searches its built-in 1000+ word dictionary and surfaces suggestions with immediate [Alt] and [Tab] hotkeys.

  • Instant Tab Completion: Type "mo" and hit Tab for "Morning", or Alt for "monitor".
  • Sub-Millisecond Keyboard Entry: No modal dialogs, no mouse hunting, no date pickers.
  • Automatic Block Sizing: Start and end times snap smoothly into the timeline ruler.

ADHD-Friendly Scheduling

Designed to bypass distraction and executive fatigue.

Silent calendar banners get lost behind browser tabs. Remind Me includes a dedicated ADHD Mode with configurable time interval check-ins (5m, 10m, 15m) and crisp audible alerts before transitions happen.

  • Pre-Activity Warnings: Audible notice before the next scheduled block starts so context switching isn't jarring.
  • Custom Audio & Volume: Use clean built-in bell chimes or load your own custom audio alert files.
  • Visual Glow Cues: Highlighting blocks in pastel yellow/amber keeps your eye anchored to the current task.

Local-Only Architecture

Runs offline. No cloud accounts. Zero tracking.

Your daily life isn't a telemetry stream. Remind Me runs entirely on your local hardware without external web servers, tracking cookies, or internet dependencies.

Works Without Wi-Fi

Launch the executable anywhere: trains, flights, or distraction-free offline setups. Zero network calls are made.

Local File Storage

Your templates and schedules are saved directly to local storage on your machine. You own your schedule data completely.

No Signups, No Accounts

Download, extract the archive, and immediately start planning. No passwords, no verification emails, no advertising SDKs.
