Fast planning for routines you actually repeat

Remind Me is not a bloated enterprise calendar. It is a focused, offline 24-hour blueprint tool built to reduce the friction of daily routine management.

Repeatable Blueprints

Save entire days as reusable templates. Stamp them onto any calendar day in a single click without repainting your week from scratch.

1000+ Word Completer

Keep your hands on the keyboard. Fast-type block names and complete activities instantly with built-in [Alt] and [Tab] shortcuts.

ADHD Interval Reminders

Toggle ADHD Mode with configurable countdown warnings (5, 10, 15 min) and customizable audio bells designed to break hyperfocus and procrastination.
