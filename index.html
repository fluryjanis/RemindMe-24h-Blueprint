Why standard calendar apps break routines

Most productivity software assumes every day is a unique blank slate. You spend 15 minutes setting up alarms and blocks, only to face the same manual drag-and-drop friction tomorrow.

Standard Productivity Suites High Friction

❌ You recreate the exact same daily structure from scratch every Monday morning.

❌ 15 clicks just to schedule wake-up, deep work, lunch, and exercise.

❌ Bloated cloud sync setups, login screens, telemetry, and endless distracting views.

❌ Missed transition cues because silent calendar popups get ignored.

Your daily schedule belongs exclusively on your personal machine. Remind Me never requires an internet connection, doesn't need an account, and contains zero third-party tracking scripts.
