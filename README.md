**Build repeatable schedules in seconds.**

Remind Me: 24h Blueprint is an **offline daily planner and routine planner** designed around speed, repeatable schedules, and reducing the friction of planning your day.

Instead of rebuilding the same routine every day, create a schedule once, save it as a template, and reuse it.

## What is Remind Me?

Remind Me is a lightweight **daily planner, schedule planner, and time-management tool** built for people who follow recurring routines.

The main idea is simple:

> **Build your routine once. Then reuse it.**

Remind Me focuses on the parts of planning that can become repetitive:

* Creating the same schedules
* Re-entering recurring tasks
* Managing time blocks
* Remembering what comes next
* Switching between planning and focused work

Rather than turning planning into another large task, Remind Me is designed to make creating a schedule fast.

---

## Features

### 🔁 Repeatable Schedules & Templates

Create a schedule once and save it as a reusable **schedule template**.

Instead of rebuilding your daily routine from scratch, reuse the same schedule whenever you need it.

This makes Remind Me particularly useful for:

* Morning routines
* School schedules
* Work schedules
* Study routines
* Workout schedules
* Project routines
* Repeating daily tasks

**Create → Save → Repeat**

---

### ⚡ Keyboard-Optimized Scheduling

Remind Me is designed around fast schedule creation.

Keyboard shortcuts and keyboard support allow you to create and edit schedules without constantly navigating menus.

The app also uses **manual word completion connected to a 1000+ word dictionary** to speed up task creation.

The goal is simple:

> **Spend less time planning and more time doing.**

---

### 📅 Single-Day Scheduling

Not every day follows the same routine.

Remind Me also supports **single-day scheduling**, allowing you to create and edit a schedule for a specific day without relying entirely on a repeating template.

This gives you both:

* Repeatable schedules for routines
* Single-day schedules for unusual days

---

### ⏱️ Time Blocks

Organize your day using **time blocking**.

Instead of treating your schedule as one long list of tasks, divide your day into meaningful blocks of time.

For example:

```text
08:00 – 09:00
Morning Routine

09:00 – 12:00
Deep Work

12:00 – 13:00
Lunch

13:00 – 17:00
Project Work

18:00 – 19:00
Exercise
```

Time blocks make it easier to see how your entire day fits together.

---

### ✅ Tasks

Add individual tasks to your schedule and organize them within your time blocks.

Remind Me combines **tasks + time blocks + reminders** so your schedule represents both what you need to do and when you intend to do it.

---

### 🔔 Reminders

Remind Me includes reminders designed to help you notice what comes next and reduce the friction of starting the next activity.

It also includes **ADHD-friendly reminders** intended to help with distraction and procrastination.

Remind Me is a planning tool and does not diagnose or treat ADHD.

---

### 🔊 Custom Sounds

Customize reminder sounds so notifications can fit your preferences.

Choose your own sounds instead of being restricted to a single default notification.

---

### 🍅 Pomodoro Timer

Remind Me includes a built-in **Pomodoro timer** so focused work can happen alongside your schedule.

Plan your day, start a focus session, and continue working without needing a separate timer.

---

### 📴 Offline

Remind Me is designed to work **offline**.

It does not require an internet connection, does not use servers, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs. Planner data is stored locally on your device.

Your operating system may still record its own system-level events according to your device's privacy settings; those events are outside the application's control.

## Why 24h Blueprint?

The name comes from thinking about the entire day as a **24-hour blueprint**.

Rather than only managing individual tasks, Remind Me lets you build a structure for the entire day:

**Time → Blocks → Tasks → Reminders → Focus**

The goal isn't to control every minute.

It's to make the structure of a recurring day easier to create and reuse.
